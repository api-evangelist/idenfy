## Website overview

Project name: iDenfy
Website: https://www.idenfy.com
Description: iDenfy is a RegTech platform for identity verification, business verification, AML screening, age estimation, and fraud prevention, helping businesses onboard users and meet KYC, KYB, and AML regulations worldwide. Customers pay only for approved verifications.

---

## Key concepts

- AI-powered identity verification (KYC) with 24/7 in-house human review
- Pay only per approved verification (denied attempts are not charged)
- Patented 3D liveness detection and passive document verification
- Business verification (KYB) with UBO screening and 180+ company registries
- AML screening and ongoing monitoring (sanctions, PEPs, watchlists, adverse media)
- Fraud prevention add-ons (face authentication, NFC, proxy/IP, phone, email, SMS)
- Coverage for 200+ countries and 16,000+ government-issued document types
- Fast integration via API, SDKs, native plugins, and no-code Magic Link
- Security-first: ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, eIDAS, GDPR, EU data residency

---

Primary audience: Regulated and fraud-exposed businesses in fintech, crypto & blockchain, iGaming, e-commerce, transportation, and other sectors that must verify identities and comply with KYC, KYB, and AML rules.
